01 / The Event

This night deserves
to be remembered.

“An event can be photographed.
But the moments people actually experience are different.”

Official event photography serves an essential purpose: it documents the stage, the speeches, and the schedule. But a photobooth captures something entirely different — how people experienced the night together.

For the College of Nursing and Allied Health Sciences, Sunset Soirée represents months of shared dedication, late-night studies, clinical rotations, and camaraderie. The unhurried smiles inside the booth become the personal keepsakes students hold onto long after the music fades.

03 / The Guest Journey

An experience designed
with rhythm and ease.

A seamless five-step interaction that balances fun spontaneity with flawless technical execution.

01

Step In

Welcoming Mirror Preview

Guests gather in front of the kiosk mirror with live framing. A simple tap on the touchscreen initiates the experience.
02

Make the Moment

8-Shot Capture Session

Guests enjoy an 8-take capture sequence with a measured countdown between frames, giving everyone ample time to change poses and laugh naturally.
03

Choose Your Frame

Sunset Soirée Artwork Selection

Guests select their favorite photos and preview them immediately inside the custom Sunset Soirée event frames.
04

Print & Scan

Dual 4R Print & Cloud QR

High-definition photographic prints emerge within seconds. Each strip features an ISO-compliant QR code for instant mobile access.
05

Take the Memory Home

Physical Keepsake & Digital 60fps BTS Video

Guests walk away holding physical prints, while their phones hold high-resolution digital copies and a fast-forward BTS timelapse video of the entire session.
